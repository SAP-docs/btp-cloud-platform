# Intelligent Scenario Management App

Manage the lifecycle of your intelligent scenarios, including training, deployment, and activation.



## Context

With this app you can manage the lifecycle of your intelligent scenarios, including training, deploying, and activating machine learning models. You can access this app from the SAP Fiori launchpad.

To use this app, you must have the `SAP_BR_ANALYTICS_SPECIALIST` business role assigned. See: [Business Catalogs and Business Roles](https://help.sap.com/docs/sap-btp-abap-environment/abap-environment/business-catalogs-and-business-roles?locale=en-US&version=Development&state=DRAFT&ai=true) 



### Key Features

You can use this app to:

-   [Deploy and Undeploy](deploy-and-undeploy-d85117a.md) a trained model for inference consumption.
-   [Activate and Deactivate](activate-and-deactivate-8a4c98c.md) a deployed model to generate predictions.
-   Monitor deployment and activation status.
-   Use [Turnkey](turnkey-d9c6d05.md) to automate all ML operations.
