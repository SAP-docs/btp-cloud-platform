# Personalizing the Home Screen

Tailor the home screen of the SAP BTP cockpit to your needs by choosing which cards are displayed and removing the ones you do not use.



## Context

The home screen is the page you see after logging on to your global account. Its content is organized in cards, such as *Favorites*, *Subaccount Quick Access*, and *What's New for the Services*. You can personalize the home screen so that it shows only the cards that are relevant to you.

Personalization is done in an edit mode that you enter from the home screen. In edit mode you can remove individual cards directly, or open a dialog to show or hide any of the available cards. Your changes apply only to your own view and take effect after you save them.



<a name="loio08d23a2705af42538cc4ab1d0f66185c__steps_personalize_home"/>

## Procedure

1.  On the home screen, choose *Edit Home*.

    The home screen switches to edit mode. Each card shows a remove icon in its top-right corner, and *Save* and *Cancel* buttons appear.

2.  To remove a card from the home screen, choose the *Remove from Home* icon \(*X*\) in the top-right corner of that card.

3.  To choose which cards are displayed, choose *Edit Cards*.

    The *Edit Cards* dialog opens, listing all available cards, each with a toggle switch:

    -   *Frequently Used Instances and Subscriptions*

    -   *Subaccount Quick Access*

    -   *Favorites*

    -   *Information Resources*

    -   *What's New for the Services*

    -   *SAP Community*

    -   *Helpful Cockpit Hints and News*


4.  In the dialog, use the toggle switch next to each card to show or hide it, and then choose *Save*.

    Choose *Cancel* to close the dialog without changing which cards are shown.

5.  Choose *Save* to apply your changes to the home screen, or *Cancel* to discard them.




<a name="loio08d23a2705af42538cc4ab1d0f66185c__result_personalize_home"/>

## Results

The home screen now displays only the cards you selected. You can return to edit mode at any time by choosing *Edit Home* to further adjust your personalization.
