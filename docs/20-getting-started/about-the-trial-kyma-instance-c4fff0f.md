# About the Trial Kyma Instance

You can try out the Kyma environment for free with restricted use of platform resources and services. A trial Kyma instance is available upon request and is intended only for personal exploration, non-productive testing, and evaluation purposes, not for production use or team development.



<a name="loioc4fff0f58f90424f8e0af28975ac7f0f__section_trial_period"/>

## Trial Period

While the SAP BTP trial account is valid for 90 days, a Kyma trial instance is valid for 30 days counting from the date it is provisioned by SAP. After 30 days, your cluster is automatically deleted, and you can no longer access your data. To continue evaluating, you can submit a new trial request. See [Getting Started with a Trial Kyma Instance](getting-started-with-a-trial-kyma-instance-ccb83c7.md).

> ### Caution:  
> Once your trial Kyma instance has expired, your cluster is deleted permanently and all the cluster resources are removed. We recommend that you back up your cluster configuration before it expires so you can restore it quickly if you request a new trial Kyma instance.



<a name="loioc4fff0f58f90424f8e0af28975ac7f0f__section_scope_limitations"/>

## Scope and Limitations

-   The trial is available upon request. For more information, see [Getting Started with a Trial Kyma Instance](getting-started-with-a-trial-kyma-instance-ccb83c7.md).
-   The maximum trial duration is 30 days.
-   You can request only one Kyma environment per global account.
-   After the first provisioning, you can't modify the list of administrators. To grant access to additional users, add and configure an additional OIDC through a custom resource.
-   A trial account provides you with a 1-node cluster with 4 vCPU and 16 GB of memory. An idle cluster without any customer workload uses around 33% of vCPU and 33% of memory.
-   There's no service level agreement with regards to the availability of the platform.



<a name="loioc4fff0f58f90424f8e0af28975ac7f0f__section_trial_expiry"/>

## Trial Expiry

You cannot extend your trial Kyma environment. After your trial Kyma instance has expired, it is automatically removed, but you request another trial instance.
